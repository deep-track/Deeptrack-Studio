Why it's structured this way

Every engagement starts with a discovery call, not a sales pitch: we need to understand the actual technical problem before we scope anything. From there, the proposal spells out deliverables, timeline, and cost in plain terms, and nothing gets built until the technical design has been reviewed and signed off.

Delivery happens with visible checkpoints rather than a single reveal at the end, and every review is measured against the real production problem, not a polished demo. When we hand over, you get documentation and full ownership, not a dependency on us to keep it running.
