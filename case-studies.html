Case Studies

The full picture behind each engagement.

Deeper detail on how each of these engagements was scoped and delivered. These are summaries of real work, not projected or hypothetical results.

AI Engineering

Math AI — Benin

Service line: AI Engineering

Overview: An applied AI system built to support mathematics learning and problem-solving. The system works through problems step by step with the student rather than simply returning answers, giving real-time guidance during the learning process.

Why it mattered: Math instruction tools that only output final answers don't teach method. This engagement required building an engineering approach that models the reasoning process, not just the result.

AI Design & Integration

Design Systems — Dallas, USA

Service line: AI Design & Integration

Overview: Built an AI-driven design system integrated directly into the client's product, Bundlesbets, giving the team a consistent, scalable UI foundation across the platform.

Why it mattered: Fast-moving product teams tend to accumulate UI inconsistency as they scale. A design system integrated with AI tooling gave this team a durable foundation instead of a one-off style guide.

Applied Research & Trust

Media CGI Detection — Mexico

Service line: Applied Research & Trust

Overview: Synthetic and CGI media detection engagement for a Mexico-based client, built to flag manipulated footage before it reaches broadcast or publication.

Why it mattered: Broadcast and publishing pipelines need a detection layer that runs before content goes out, not after damage is already done. This work sits squarely in Deeptrack's core applied-research territory: synthetic media trust and verification.

AI Engineering

Language Translation — Japan

Service line: AI Engineering

Overview: An Amharic–Swahili–Japanese translation system built for Mitsumi, enabling accurate three-way communication across language pairs that weren't previously supported.

Why it mattered: Off-the-shelf translation tooling rarely covers this specific combination of languages well. The engagement required building for language pairs that generalist vendors don't prioritize.

Applied Research & Trust

Deepfake Symposium — Singapore

Service line: Applied Research & Trust

Overview: A research and speaking engagement on deepfakes and synthetic media, sharing applied detection findings with an international research audience.

Why it mattered: This is the kind of thought-leadership work that keeps the team's applied research current, and it directly informs the detection approaches used in client engagements like the Mexico CGI detection work above.

AI Engineering

Async Agent API Service — UAE

Service line: AI Engineering

Overview: Design and implementation of an asynchronous agent API service for a UAE-based client, built to handle long-running agent tasks reliably at scale.

Why it mattered: Long-running AI agent tasks break simple request/response API patterns. This required an architecture built around async job handling, not a bolt-on to a synchronous system.

AI Consulting & Strategy

AI Readiness for CEOs — Kenya

Service line: AI Consulting & Strategy

Overview: Executive AI-readiness advisory, delivered in partnership with Centric Essentials Consulting, helping leadership teams set a clear, practical AI adoption roadmap.

Why it mattered: Executive teams often default to either AI skepticism or overinvestment without a structured readiness assessment. This engagement gave leadership a build-vs-buy framework grounded in their actual technical capacity.
